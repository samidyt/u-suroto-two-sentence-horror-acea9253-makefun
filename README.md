## The entry

**"The census form asked how many people live in my house. I wrote one, and it came back corrected to two."** (103 characters, exactly two sentences)

## Why this story

Against the judge's criteria (in the brief's own order):

1. **Genuinely frightening, exactly two sentences** — an unseen
   presence lives in the narrator's house and can read, handle, and
   correct their mail.
2. **The second sentence turns the first** — a boring census form
   becomes proof of a second occupant; on a second read, "came back
   corrected" means something in the house answered for itself.
3. **Implied, not explained** — no monster is named, no gore, no
   chase; the reader fills in the worst of it.
4. **Precise and concrete** — a census form, "wrote one", "corrected
   to two". Every word earns its place.
5. **Original** — no census-form entry among the 200 visible
   submissions, and it avoids the crowded clusters (smart speakers,
   sleep recordings, lullabies, childhood photos, hospice, mirrors,
   shoes by the door, waving neighbours).
